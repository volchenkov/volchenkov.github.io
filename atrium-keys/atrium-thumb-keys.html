Screen calibration

bank card — 85.6 × 54 mm

Hold a bank card against the screen and drag until the outline matches it. Key widths in millimetres then come out right.

Second row

Atrium is polyphonic, so both rows can share one channel. Give them separate channels to drive two timbres at once.

Playing

Internal sound

A plain two-saw voice for practising without the synth plugged in. Turn it off once Atrium is connected.

Connecting Atrium

USB-C cable from the phone to Atrium’s USB-C port — the phone is the USB host, Atrium appears as a class-compliant MIDI device. Open this page in Chrome (Web MIDI is not in Firefox or Safari) and allow MIDI access when asked. For the 3.5 mm TRS MIDI input, use a USB MIDI interface with a Type A adapter.
